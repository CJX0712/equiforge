# Model Card — EquiFuse

This model card follows a condensed version of Mitchell et al. (2019),
"Model Cards for Model Reporting".

## Model details

* **Name:** EquiFuse
* **Authors:** 晨星 (Morning Star)
* **Version:** 0.1.0
* **Type:** Binary classifier + fairness postprocessor (group fairness).
* **Base estimator:** Logistic regression (scikit-learn or pure-NumPy IRLS).
* **Fairness interventions:** Reweighing (Kamiran & Calders, 2012) +
  Equalized-Odds postprocessing (Hardt et al., 2016, randomised thresholds).
* **License:** MIT

## Intended use

* **Primary use:** Reduce equalized-odds disparity across a binary protected
  attribute in tabular binary classification, with a *hard, pre-registered*
  fairness–accuracy guarantee.
* **Users:** ML practitioners auditing or deploying classifiers where regulatory
  or ethical group-fairness constraints apply.
* **Out of scope:** Non-binary labels, continuous fairness notions, causal
  fairness, or deployment without human oversight. The synthetic benchmark is a
  *proxy*; real deployment requires validation on the actual population.

## Training data

EquiFuse is evaluated on **synthetic** datasets generated by
`data/synthesizer.py`. Each dataset has a protected attribute `s`, a single
merit feature `z` (the only real signal), `n_noise` uninformative features, and a
label with controlled base-rate gap (`group_gap`), direct group effect
(`direct_effect`), and label noise (`label_noise`):

* `credit_like` — `group_gap=0.9, direct_effect=0.35` (large base-rate gap).
* `adult_like` — `group_gap=0.7, direct_effect=0.35` (moderate, robust EO signal).
* `law_school_like` — `group_gap=1.0, direct_effect=0.20` (strong gap, mild direct effect).

These are *toy scenarios* for reproducible research, **not** real-world data.
The generator is fully seeded and reproducible.

## Evaluation

Aggregate (mean across datasets, `seeds = [7, 11, 23]`, `n = 4000`):

| Method | ACC | DPD | EOD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| unconstrained | 0.810 | 0.333 | 0.136 |
| reweighing | 0.810 | 0.333 | 0.136 |
| eo_postprocess | 0.799 | 0.262 | 0.065 |
| dp_postprocess | 0.775 | 0.124 | 0.081 |
| **equifuse** | **0.800** | **0.257** | **0.062** |

**Pre-registered gate (per dataset, all pass):**

| Dataset | Unconstrained EOD → EquiFuse EOD | Unconstrained ACC → EquiFuse ACC | Pass |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| credit_like | 0.134 → 0.060 | 0.799 → 0.789 | ✅ |
| adult_like | 0.091 → 0.045 | 0.809 → 0.800 | ✅ |
| law_school_like | 0.183 → 0.082 | 0.821 → 0.812 | ✅ |

EquiFuse cuts equalized-odds disparity by roughly **55%** while keeping accuracy
within **~1 point** of the unconstrained baseline. Two identical runs are
bit-identical (determinism verified).

## Limitations & bias

* **Synthetic-only validation.** Results are demonstrated on controlled
  synthetic data. Real populations may exhibit larger direct effects, intersectional
  unfairness, and distribution shift that this toolkit does not model.
* **Binary groups.** The targeted equalisation is implemented and validated for
  two protected groups; the >2-group path falls back to per-group accuracy-optimal
  thresholds (no cross-group equalisation).
* **Trade-off is real.** When the true disparity is small, aggressive
  equalisation can increase *measured* disparity on finite samples and costs
  accuracy. EquiFuse targets a safe `0.45 × baseline` reduction; operators should
  still review per-dataset gate results.
* **Postprocessing uses `s` at inference.** This is standard for Hardt et al.
  (2016) postprocessing, but it requires access to the protected attribute at
  serving time, which may be restricted in some jurisdictions.
* **No causal claims.** EquiFuse equalises *statistical* error rates; it does not
  establish or repair the causal sources of disparity.

## Ethical considerations

EquiFuse is a *mitigation* tool, not an audit. A low EOD does not certify a model
as fair in any legal sense. It should be used alongside broader impact assessment,
stakeholder review, and monitoring in production.
